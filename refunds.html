1. In-store purchases

All ice cream, bakery items, and beverages are made and sold in person at our Ball Ground location. Because these are perishable, made-to-order items, we generally do not offer refunds once an item has been prepared and served.

2. Quality issues

If something about your order was wrong or didn't meet our quality standards, please let a team member know before you leave, or contact us within 24 hours. We will make it right with a replacement or store credit whenever we reasonably can.

3. Catering & large orders

Catering and large-order deposits, when applicable, are discussed and confirmed directly with our team at the time of booking. Cancellation and refund terms for these orders will be communicated in writing before the deposit is collected.

4. How to request a refund or correction

To request a refund or flag a quality issue, call us at (678) 454-2256 or use our contact form with your visit date and order details. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
